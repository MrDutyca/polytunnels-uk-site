About

Independent commercial polytunnel specialists

Polytunnels-UK installs, repairs, re-sheets, refurbishes and alters commercial polytunnels and canopies. We work with structures from different manufacturers and periods, allowing practical solutions to be based on the site and the work required rather than on selling one proprietary system.

The emphasis is on clear assessment, safe working, minimal disruption and extending the useful life of existing structures.

Working area

Quotation process

A quick route from enquiry to quotation

  1. Enter the approximate tunnel length, width and preferred sheet type, where known.
  2. Tell us whether anything is unsafe, exposed to the weather or putting crops, plants or livestock at risk.
  3. Add photographs if available. A full view and close photographs of any damage or sheet fixings are useful.
  4. We will acknowledge the enquiry during the same normal working day and contact you for anything else needed.
  5. Once sufficient information is available, an initial assessment is provided within 48 hours.
