About

Independent commercial polytunnel specialists

Polytunnels-UK installs, repairs, re-sheets, refurbishes and alters commercial polytunnels and canopies.

We work with structures of all ages and from all manufacturers, allowing us to provide practical solutions tailored to your specific needs. We are not affiliated to any one manufacturer, so our advice is based solely on what is best for you and your site.

Our emphasis is on clear communication, best working practices and minimal disruption, while maximising the benefits to your business.

Working area

England and Wales service area

Polytunnels-UK works across England and Wales, primarily from the South Coast northwards to approximately Cheshire, Derbyshire, Nottinghamshire and North Lincolnshire, including the Isle of Wight and excluding the Isle of Man.

Commercial work outside the primary area is considered according to project size, timing and logistics.

Quotation process

  1. Send the organisation name, site postcode, required work and available photographs.
